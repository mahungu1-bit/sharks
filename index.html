About Sharks

Sharks are fascinating marine animals that have lived in our oceans for hundreds of millions of years. They come in many shapes and sizes and play an important role in keeping ocean ecosystems healthy.

🦈 Amazing Shark Facts

🦈 Great White Shark

A powerful predator known for its size and sharp teeth.

🌊 Whale Shark

The largest fish in the world. It feeds mainly on tiny organisms.

⚡ Hammerhead Shark

Recognized by its unique hammer-shaped head and wide-set eyes.
